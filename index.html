Lindy lead

Lead the couple in time to the music. Pick a tempo to start (the first tap also unlocks the sound).

S (left thumb / key S) and C (right thumb / key C). Press near an even count (2, 4, 6, 8); the move starts on the next count. While a swing out plays, press C on its count 4 for an outside turn. Every press should be on a count: a stray press resets your chain. Esc or "menu" leaves a song.

Landscape works best on a phone. Wear headphones or use the speaker, then calibrate once per device.
